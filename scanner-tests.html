PFF / Scanner tests

Same objects, different scanners

Every 3D scanner I test scans the same three objects. Pick any two scans and compare them side by side in 3D. The full reviews are on YouTube.

  • 01

    Anatomical skull

    Organic shapes: smooth freeform surfaces plus deep eye sockets and the nasal cavity, which show how far a scanner can reach into cavities.

  • 02

    Scan Bench

    Mechanical parts: the Scan Bench, “the Benchy for 3D scanners”, with sharp edges, holes, thin walls and small text.

  • 03

    Raspberry Pi

    Fine detail and demanding surfaces, without scan spray: reflective ports, connectors and the printing on the PCB.

All scans of a test are aligned to the same coordinate system in QuickSurface, so they open in the same orientation. Where needed, meshes were reduced to just under 4 million triangles to fit Sketchfab's upload limit.
