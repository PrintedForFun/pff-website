Privacy Policy

1. Controller

The controller responsible for data processing on this website is:
Philipp Kreiser, PrintedForFun – for address and contact details see the Legal Notice.

2. General

This website is a purely static site. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking tools, and does not embed any third-party fonts, scripts or content (such as videos).

3. Hosting via GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the website, GitHub collects technically necessary data in server log files, in particular your IP address, the date and time of access, the page requested, and browser and operating system information. This data is required to deliver the website and to ensure its security.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; our legitimate interest lies in providing the website securely and reliably. Data may be transferred to the USA; GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More information: GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. Domain and DNS via Cloudflare

This website's domain is managed through Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the website, the domain is resolved via Cloudflare's name servers; if traffic is routed through Cloudflare, Cloudflare processes technically necessary connection data such as your IP address. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More information: Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

5. Contact by email

If you contact us by email, the information you provide (email address, name, content of the message) is stored and processed to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where your enquiry relates to a contract, and otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. The data is deleted once it is no longer needed to handle your enquiry, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

Emails sent to addresses at printedforfun.com are received by Cloudflare Email Routing (Cloudflare, Inc., see section 4) and forwarded to a mailbox at Gmail, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, where they are stored. Cloudflare only forwards the messages and does not keep their content. Data may be transferred to the USA in the process; both Cloudflare and Google are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More information: Google Privacy Policy.

6. Links to social networks

This website contains plain links to our profiles on YouTube, Reddit and Facebook. These are not embedded plugins; no data is transmitted to these networks when you visit this website. Only when you click a link are you taken to the respective provider's site, where its own privacy policy applies.

7. Your rights

Within the scope of the applicable law, you have the right at any time to:

  • access the data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) and restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • object to processing based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR)

To exercise these rights, simply email contact@printedforfun.com.
